# RisparmiAi Privacy Notice

**Updated on 17 October 2026 — version 2026-10-17.1**

## At a glance

- No account is required: we do not ask for a name, email or password to use the app.
- Saved prices and custom categories are stored on your device.
- Online analysis sends chat text and necessary context to OpenAI or Google Gemini, depending on the app configuration.
- Google AdMob may process device and interaction data for ads. Advertising choices are separate from acknowledging this notice.

## 1. Privacy contact

For information and requests: [roadtogethere@gmail.com](mailto:roadtogethere@gmail.com).

## 2. Local data

RisparmiAi compares prices and quantities. It does not require login, camera, contacts or GPS location. Prices you explicitly save, their attributes and custom categories remain in the app's local storage. Chat stays in the session; relevant context may be reused in later AI requests.

Local storage also holds interstitial frequency counters, the last ad timestamp and the notice version you viewed. The app does not automatically send the entire saved-price archive to AdMob.

## 3. Online analysis and AI services

When you request online analysis, the app sends your message and necessary context directly over HTTPS to the configured provider, **OpenAI or Google Gemini**: descriptions, prices, quantities, attributes, categories and relevant conversation content. The catalog may include custom category schemas. Providers also receive technical connection information, such as your IP address. Demo analysis is simulated without these AI requests.

Free text is not automatically anonymised: do not enter personal or health data, credentials or other confidential information. We do not use chat or price history to populate advertising targeting requests.

Provider retention and further uses depend on the service, contracts and account configuration used by the app. We do not promise immediate deletion or no third-party retention. See [OpenAI API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) and [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

## 4. Google AdMob advertising

AdMob serves banners and interstitials. The SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers, technical and diagnostic data, app launches and ad interactions. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, security and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on the platform, settings and applicable choices.

When required, Google UMP presents an advertising choices form. You can change choices through **Preferenze privacy annunci** on the saved prices page when available. Acknowledging this notice is not consent to personalised advertising; non-personalised ads may also involve technical processing.

See [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 5. Purposes, legal bases and transfers

Processing necessary for price comparisons and requested analysis serves to provide the service requested by the user. Advertising activities requiring consent depend on choices collected through UMP. Other technical processing must have an applicable legal basis, according to the parties' roles and notices. The app does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their service terms and safeguards apply, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where relevant. You may ask the privacy contact for details.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you clear app data in device settings. Uninstallation, backups and restoration depend on the operating system and device or account settings. Selective deletion of saved prices is not currently available in the app.

Connections to the services mentioned use HTTPS; we do not claim additional encryption of local storage. Local deletion does not automatically delete data already received by providers: their notices, retention periods and support channels also apply.

## 7. Rights and updates

Where provided by law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. You may complain to the [Italian Data Protection Authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/). Write to [roadtogethere@gmail.com](mailto:roadtogethere@gmail.com); the lack of an account may limit identification of data held by third parties.

You can always reopen this notice through the privacy icon in the chat. New versions are shown again at startup. **I've read it, continue** stores only the viewed version on your device, not blanket consent to processing or advertising.
